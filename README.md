# aquarium-manager
Manage Aquarium: 
- Database to store fish, invertebrates, plants, etc. for your own aquarium.
- Every time a fish is added to the database, a pixel art of the fish will be added to your virtual "tank" (same goes to others like plants and invertebrates).
- You can add notes to certain fishes/inverts/plants (current health status, active diseases, personal notes, etc.)
- You can add notifications to feed your fishes, add more fertilizing liquid for your plants, or changing the water.
- You can record information about your tank when starting up your account (dimensions) to make your virtual tank more realistic to your own tank.
- You can have a wishlist of new things you want to buy later on when you go shopping for new things.
- Added fishes/plants/inverts will "grow" over time by collecting data from the database scheduled at reasonable time intervals.
- Add login functions (authentication) for multiple users to access their accounts.
- Add connection to database.
- Fish can be drawn by yourself manually (open pixel art box for you to personalize and draw your fishes, name them too).
- Manage/store other test about the tank (PH, nitritel, etc.)
- Graphs/visualizations to track tank and fish wellbeing.
